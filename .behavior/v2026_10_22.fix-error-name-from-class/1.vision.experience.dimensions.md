# 1.vision.experience.dimensions

the space every experience of this change sits in. scope: helpful-errors leaves `error.name` alone, and adds a rule plus a clamp that forbid any override.

| axis | positions | what it varies |
|---|---|---|
| actor | contributor to helpful-errors · consumer who classifies in process · producer who classifies across a wire · consumer who switches on `name` · consumer who upgrades | who meets the invariant, and what they do with an error |
| class | `HelpfulError` (base) · a built-in (`ConstraintError`, `MalfunctionError`, `BadRequestError`, `UnexpectedCodePathError`) · a consumer subclass (`QuoteExpiredError extends ConstraintError`) | which `new.target` the constructor sees; whether inheritance matters to the reader |

## orthogonality

- **actor ⟂ class.** every actor can meet every class; no actor implies a class. one caveat: a contributor to this repo does not author consumer subclasses, but the clamp holds one as a fixture, so that cell is real
- the axes generate 5 × 3 = 15 cells. `1.vision.experience.case=_.md` walks each one

## collapses

- **class collapses for every actor but one.** `name` reads `'Error'` for all three classes, so the outcome per actor is class-invariant. the exception: the producer across a wire, where a consumer subclass is the case that proves class detection follows inheritance (`instanceof ConstraintError` is true for `QuoteExpiredError`) and a flat `name` match would not

## the dimension this removed

the prior scope carried a **bundle** axis (names kept vs mangled by a minifier). under the new scope `name` never reads the class, so a mangled class name leaves what `name` reports unchanged. the axis is gone, and the archived catalog at `archive/scope=name-equals-class/` keeps it
